About the Center for Hybrid Intelligence
The Center for Hybrid Intelligence (CHI) is a research center at Aarhus University dedicated to ensuring human expertise remains central as artificial intelligence transforms work, organizations, and society. Combining research across education, organizational design, and public policy, CHI develops frameworks — including the FERC Framework and the 4P Framework — that help individuals, companies, and institutions use AI in ways that strengthen rather than erode human judgment.
