## Q19 Does relying on AI tools affect people's critical thinking?

*risks* · 33 s (first words after 6 s)

### Answer

Yes, research shows that relying heavily on AI tools can negatively affect people's critical thinking skills. Studies found that frequent AI use leads to "cognitive offloading," where people let the AI do the thinking instead of using their own brains [AI Tools in Society: Impacts on Cognitive Offloading and the Future of Critical Thinking, p. 15]. For example, a survey of 666 people showed that higher AI usage was strongly linked to lower critical thinking scores, even when controlling for education level [AI Tools in Society: Impacts on Cognitive Offloading and the Future of Critical Thinking, p. 15-17]. Other studies support this by showing that over-reliance on AI can shrink the brain's neural connections needed for deep analytical work [Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 122-124]. While AI can help with speed and basic tasks, it may prevent users from developing strong independent judgment or problem-solving abilities [Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of Artificial Intelligence on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality, p. 18].
