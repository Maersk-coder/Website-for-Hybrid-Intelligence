## Q13 How much will AI increase economic productivity over the next ten years?

*disagreement (modest estimate vs. big forecasts)* · 39 s (first words after 12 s)

### Answer

Research papers estimate that AI could increase global economic productivity (Total Factor Productivity) by about 0.66% to 1.56% over the next ten years, though some experts predict much higher figures like 300%. [The Simple Macroeconomics of AI, p. 2-3] One major study suggests a modest gain of only 0.53%, while another argues that if AI requires massive investment changes, the boost could reach up to 1.4% to 1.56%. [The Simple Macroeconomics of AI, p. 45] However, these numbers depend on assumptions about how fast technology costs will drop and how widely AI is used.

## Q14 Does using ChatGPT to write essays affect how people learn?

*risks* · 31 s (first words after 11 s)

### Answer

Using ChatGPT to write essays can hinder how people learn by making students less likely to remember information and think deeply about the material [Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 19-20]. Studies show that relying too much on AI for writing reduced students' ability to create something creative and made it harder for them to keep what they learned [Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 12; Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 44-45]. Some researchers also say this use of AI can lower confidence in one's own writing skills [Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 12-13].

## Q15 Is AI good or bad for workers?

*balanced answer across several papers* · 32 s (first words after 12 s)

### Answer

AI can be helpful but also harmful for workers because it boosts productivity for some while hurting performance in others [Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of Artificial Intelligence on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality, p. 18]. Some studies show that AI leads to big skill gains and fewer job losses, especially for new or less experienced staff [Generative AI at Work, p. 23], while other research finds it might increase inequality or lower wages for certain groups [The Simple Macroeconomics of AI, p. 46].
