## Q18 What are the environmental costs of large language models?

*risks, specific facts* · 75 s (first words after 39 s)

### Answer

Large language models use a lot of energy and create carbon emissions, which hurts the environment more as the models get bigger [On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots: Can Language Models Be Too Big?, p. 1-2]. These systems can consume millions of dollars in computing costs to train alone, and running them continues to burn energy even after they are built [Generative AI at Work, p. 7-8]. The environmental damage often falls on marginalized communities who may not benefit from the technology [On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots: Can Language Models Be Too Big?, p. 3-4]. For example, using an LLM for a single query uses about ten times more energy than using a standard search engine like Google [Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 152-153].

## Q19 Does relying on AI tools affect people's critical thinking?

*risks* · 35 s (first words after 14 s)

### Answer

Yes, relying on AI tools generally lowers people's ability to think critically, as studies show that more use of these tools is linked to worse critical thinking scores [AI Tools in Society: Impacts on Cognitive Offloading and the Future of Critical Thinking, p. 16]. This happens because using AI causes people to offload mental work, which reduces their practice in deep analysis [AI Tools in Society: Impacts on Cognitive Offloading and the Future of Critical Thinking, p. 23]. However, having a higher level of education can help protect some critical thinking skills even when using AI frequently [AI Tools in Society: Impacts on Cognitive Offloading and the Future of Critical Thinking, p. 15].

## Q20 What are the main risks of large language models like ChatGPT?

*balanced answer across several papers* · 47 s (first words after 13 s)

### Answer

Large language models can spread harmful biases from the internet they were trained on and make people feel more trusting in untrue information [On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots: Can Language Models Be Too Big?, p. 7]. They can also be used by bad actors to create fake texts for term papers or to recruit people into extremist groups [On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots: Can Language Models Be Too Big?, p. 10]. In school settings, using these tools might stop students from learning deeply because they do not have to think or write anything themselves [Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 20]. Additionally, companies making these models pay huge costs for electricity and computing power that often do not benefit everyone directly [On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots: Can Language Models Be Too Big?, p. 10].
