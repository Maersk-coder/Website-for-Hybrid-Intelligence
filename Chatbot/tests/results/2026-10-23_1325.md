## Q11 How much did an AI assistant help customer support workers, and who benefited most?

*specific facts* · 46 s (first words after 13 s)

### Answer

The research shows that generative AI tools helped customer support workers resolve about 15% more issues per hour on average [Generative AI at Work, p. 2]. Workers who benefited the most were those with less experience and lower skill levels; for example, newer agents saw a roughly 30% increase in the number of problems they could fix each hour [Generative AI at Work, p. 3-5]. In contrast, very skilled or experienced workers did not see large gains in speed, and some actually produced slightly lower quality conversations when using the tool [Generative AI at Work, p. 3-5]. These benefits were strongest for agents who followed the AI's suggestions closely [Generative AI at Work, p. 23-24].

## Q12 What is the "jagged technological frontier"?

*definition* · 42 s (first words after 12 s)

### Answer

The "jagged technological frontier" describes how AI's capabilities are uneven, sometimes excelling at simple tasks while struggling with complex ones like counting errors in text. This means experts often perform worse when using AI because they rely on it too much or get tricked by its mistakes. [Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of Artificial Intelligence on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality, p. 18-19]

The jagged nature suggests that AI acts as a mix of a genius and a confused student, depending on the specific task. This happens even in real-world jobs where workers aren't relying on AI for every part of their work. [Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of Artificial Intelligence on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality, p. 18-19]

## Q13 How much will AI increase economic productivity over the next ten years?

*disagreement (modest estimate vs. big forecasts)* · 41 s (first words after 14 s)

### Answer

Based on the provided research, experts estimate that AI could increase total factor productivity by less than 0.53% to 0.66% over the next decade [The Simple Macroeconomics of AI, p. 2-3]. While some earlier reports predicted much larger gains, this paper argues those numbers may be exaggerated because future "harder" tasks are harder for AI to master currently [The Simple Macroeconomics of AI, p. 45-47]. ## Q14 Does using ChatGPT to write essays affect how people learn?

*risks* · 65 s (first words after 17 s)

### Answer

Yes, research shows that relying on tools like ChatGPT can reduce a student's need for intellectual effort and long-term learning [Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 20-21]. Using the AI can make students less able to recall information from their own work and weaker at remembering details they wrote [Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 31-34]. While some people found it easy to write quickly with the help of ChatGPT, others felt it made them lazy or like they were cheating [Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 44-45]. The studies suggest that while AI helps with writing speed and structure, it can sometimes hurt critical thinking skills unless students are taught how to use it carefully [Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt when Using an AI Assistant for Essay Writing Task, p. 20-21].

## Q15 Is AI good or bad for workers?

*balanced answer across several papers* · 52 s (first words after 21 s)

### Answer

AI has mixed effects on workers because it can increase their productivity but also lead to higher inequality [The Simple Macroeconomics of AI, p. 29]. While some experts are optimistic that new tasks might help lower-skill workers and raise wages, others warn that automation could displace labor and widen the gap between skilled and unskilled workers [The Simple Macroeconomics of AI, p. 18]. The net result depends on whether AI creates enough good jobs to offset the displacement of old ones.
