## Q19 Does relying on AI tools affect people's critical thinking?

*risks* · 63 s (first words after 28 s)

### Answer

Research shows that relying heavily on AI tools is linked to lower critical thinking skills because it can cause people to offload cognitive tasks instead of engaging deeply [AI Tools in Society: Impacts on Cognitive Offloading and the Future of Critical Thinking, p. 16]. This negative trend is strongest for younger users who depend more on these tools, while higher education levels may help protect some of their critical thinking abilities [AI Tools in Society: Impacts on Cognitive Offloading and the Future of Critical Thinking, p. 15]. However, other studies suggest AI can also boost productivity and performance in specific tasks like coding or route-finding when used correctly [Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of Artificial Intelligence on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality, p. 18; Generative AI at Work, p. 6].
