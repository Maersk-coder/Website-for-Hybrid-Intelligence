## Q5 When does combining humans and AI work best?

*conditions* · 37 s (first words after 12 s)

### Answer

Combining humans and AI works best in tasks that involve creating content rather than making decisions, as research shows AI tends to cause performance losses when helping with decisions but gains when aiding creative work [When combinations of humans and AI are useful: A systematic review and meta-analysis, p. 3]. This pattern occurs because while humans excel at reasoning through diverse problems, they often struggle with the repetitive details that AI can handle quickly [When combinations of humans and AI are useful: A systematic review and meta-analysis, p. 2]. Additionally, combining intelligence works best when there is synergy, meaning the team performs better than either person working alone, which requires carefully designed processes where each partner handles subtasks they are best at [Hybrid Intelligence, p. 5]. For example, studies suggest generative tasks like creating images or text benefit most from this kind of collaboration [When combinations of humans and AI are useful: A systematic review and meta-analysis, p. 6].
