## Q1 What is Hybrid Intelligence?

*definition* · 53 s (first words after 41 s)

### Answer

On average, humans working with AI perform worse than using either the human alone or the best AI alone [When combinations of humans and AI are useful: A systematic review and meta-analysis, p. 1-2].
